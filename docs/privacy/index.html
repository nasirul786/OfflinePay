Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: OfflinePay has no accounts and no servers. Your UPI PIN is never stored. Your payment history stays on your phone. Payment details go only to your bank, through NPCI's *99# service, to make the payment you asked for.

1. About this policy

This policy explains what information the OfflinePay Android app ("OfflinePay", "the app", "we") handles, why, and what choices you have. OfflinePay is open source and is distributed as an APK on GitHub under the package name com.bottercc.offlinepay. You can read its source code to check everything described here.

OfflinePay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI), any bank or any mobile operator.

2. How OfflinePay works

OfflinePay makes UPI payments using *99#, NPCI's USSD service. When you start a payment, the app dials *99# on your phone and fills in each step of your bank's USSD menu for you. These USSD messages travel over your mobile network to your operator, NPCI and your bank, the same way they would if you dialled *99# yourself. OfflinePay does not run any server and does not receive copies of these messages.

3. Information we never collect or store

  • Your UPI PIN. You enter it on OfflinePay's keypad. It is held in memory only for the payment you are making, typed into your bank's *99# dialog, and discarded when that session ends. It is never written to storage, logged or sent anywhere else.
  • Debit card details. When you link a bank account, you type your card details directly into your bank's *99# dialog. OfflinePay does not read or keep them.
  • Accounts or sign-in details. OfflinePay has no user accounts.
  • Your contacts, photos, location or messages. OfflinePay does not ask for these.

4. Information stored only on your phone

To make the app useful, OfflinePay saves the following in its private storage on your device. It never leaves your phone through OfflinePay.

5. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Accessibility Service: used only to read and answer the *99# USSD dialog during a payment or request you started. While no session is running, the service listens only to OfflinePay's own screens and ignores all other apps. It does not collect, store or share any on-screen content beyond what is needed to complete your *99# session.
  • Phone (CALL_PHONE): to dial *99#.
  • Phone state (READ_PHONE_STATE): to list the SIM cards in your phone so the payment uses the SIM registered with your bank.
  • Camera: only while the QR scanner is open, to read UPI QR codes. Images are processed on your phone and are not saved or uploaded.
  • Internet and network state: OfflinePay itself does not use the internet. These permissions are added by Google's ML Kit library, which powers the QR scanner (see section 6).

6. Third-party services

Google ML Kit (QR scanning)

The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit's barcode scanning. The scanning model is built into the app, so QR codes are read on your phone and camera images are not sent to Google. When your phone has an internet connection, ML Kit may contact Google to receive updates and to send anonymous information about the performance and use of the scanner. This does not include camera images, payment details or your PIN. See Google's ML Kit terms and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your bank, NPCI and your mobile operator

When you make a payment, the details you enter (such as the payee, amount, note and UPI PIN) are sent through the *99# service to your bank, NPCI and your mobile operator so they can process it. Their own privacy policies apply to that processing.

GitHub and this website

The app is downloaded from GitHub, and this website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information about visits and downloads under its own privacy statement. The app itself has no analytics or crash reporting.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we do not show ads. Apart from what is described in section 6, no information is sent from the app.

8. Security

Information saved by OfflinePay is stored in the app's private storage, which Android protects from other apps. Your PIN is never saved. Please keep your phone locked and never share your UPI PIN with anyone.

9. Children

OfflinePay is meant for people who hold a bank account linked to UPI. It is not directed at children under 18.

10. Your choices

  • You can turn off OfflinePay's Accessibility Service at any time in Android Settings. Payments then cannot be automated.
  • You can deny the camera permission and still pay by mobile number, UPI ID or bank account.
  • You can delete all stored information by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

11. Changes to this policy
