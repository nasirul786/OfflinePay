2. What OfflinePay is

OfflinePay is an interface for *99#, the USSD service for UPI run by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI). The app dials *99# on your phone and fills in your bank's menus for you.

  • OfflinePay is not a bank, payment system operator, payment aggregator or wallet. It does not hold, move or receive money.
  • Payments are processed by your bank, NPCI and the payee's bank, and are sent over your mobile operator's network.

3. Who can use it

You must be at least 18 years old and hold a bank account linked to UPI, with a mobile number registered with your bank. You must use the app only with your own bank account, SIM and UPI PIN.

4. Your responsibilities

  • Check the payee, amount and account before entering your UPI PIN. Payments made through UPI generally cannot be reversed by OfflinePay.
  • Keep your UPI PIN secret. Never enter it to receive money.
  • Use the app only for lawful payments, and follow your bank's and NPCI's rules.
  • Keep the Accessibility Service turned on only if you want OfflinePay to complete *99# steps for you.

5. Limits and charges

  • *99# payments are limited to ₹5,000 per transaction. Your bank may set lower or daily limits.
  • OfflinePay is free. Your mobile operator may charge for each *99# session, currently capped by TRAI at ₹0.50. Your bank may apply its own charges.

We do not guarantee that every payment will complete, or that the app will always be available.

If the app shows an unclear result, check the SMS from your bank or your bank statement before trying again, so you don't pay twice.
